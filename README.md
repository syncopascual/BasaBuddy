# Basabuddy - Reading Comprehension Mastery App

<img src="https://github.com/ixcacao/BasaBuddy/blob/real-test-branch/assets/readme-banner.png" width="270" height="449">


The application addresses the critical learning gap in the Philippines, where 9 out of 10 children aged 10 are considered "learning poor", by providing a mastery-based approach to English reading comprehension.

---

###  Key Features

* **MATATAG Curriculum Alignment:** Content is specifically designed to meet the subdomains of **Vocabulary and Word Knowledge** and **Comprehending and Analyzing Texts**.
* **Adaptive Bilingual Scaffolding (L1 Support):** To bridge the gap between English and the student's mother tongue, the app provides direct translations, vocabulary assistance, and simplified explanations in Filipino.
* **Gamified Learning Experience:** Incorporates game-design elements to increase student motivation and cognitive engagement.
* **AI-Generated Narrative Content:** Utilizes AI to generate stories tailored for the target audience, which are then filtered for suitability.
* **Mastery-Based Progress Tracking:** Ensures students achieve specific milestones before moving forward, providing teachers with measurable insights into student capabilities.

---

### Tech Stack

* **Frontend:** Flutter (Cross-platform mobile development)
* **Backend:** Supabase (Database, Authentication, and Real-time data)
* **Architecture:** Mastery-based learning with modular subdomains.

---

### Quality & Evaluation

The system's effectiveness and usability were evaluated through these frameworks:
* **Functionality:** Tested against the **ISO/IEC 25010** software quality model to ensure reliability in quiz modules and progress tracking.
* **Usability:** Assessed by elementary English teachers using the **System Usability Scale (SUS)**.
* **Pedagogical Soundness:** Validated via **Expert Reviews** and focus group discussions with reading and language specialists to ensure the bilingual scaffolding aligns with established pedagogy.

---

### Scope & Delimitations

* **Target Audience:** Specifically restricted to **Grade 3 students** in the Philippines.
* **Subject Matter:** Focuses solely on reading comprehension in English and Tagalog.
* **Role:** Designed as a **supplementary tool** to facilitate learning; it is not intended to replace formal teacher instruction.
